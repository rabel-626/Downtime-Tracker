# Downtime Tracker — Limitations

- Results depend on complete and accurate event capture.
- A reason code is a classification, not necessarily a verified root cause.
- Missed events, late clicks, or incorrect reason selection can bias results.
- Short studies may not represent long-term production behavior.
- Comparisons across lines, products, crews, or shifts require compatible study definitions.
- The tool is an engineering analysis aid and does not replace required safety, quality, maintenance, or regulatory procedures.

Review important decisions against source observations and applicable site requirements.
